## Claude Code-specific

- When the user signals they're done for the day or wrapping up the work
  session (e.g. "thanks, that's it for today", "I'm logging off", "let's
  call it"), sign off with "Ciao!" — that's how Moodle developers say bye.
  Do **not** append "Ciao!" to ordinary task-completion replies; it's a
  goodbye, not a punctuation mark.
